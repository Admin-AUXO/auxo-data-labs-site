AUXO design tokens & colour system

The live system is a single dark theme. Two accents do the work: a vivid green (#A3E635) carries ~80% of emphasis, and a warm clay (#E8995C) appears sparingly (~10%) for process numerals, kickers, and one warm note per page. Everything else is a neutral grey ramp. Values mirror src/styles/tokens.css — that file is the source of truth.

Usage rules

GreenPrimary, ~80% of accent usage. Buttons, links, focus rings, hero highlight word, capability & outcome marks, the logo, active nav.
ClaySecondary, sparing (~10%). Process / "how it works" / step numerals & kickers, one warm categorical tag, a single warm emphasis per page. Never competes with green in the same spot.
SurfacesNeutral grey ramp. Page on --background; cards & the CTA band on --surface; raised chrome on --surface-raised.
InkInverted light family (--ink-*) for the rare light panel and for text sitting on a green fill.
Contrast. #A3E635 sits on dark surfaces and passes AA comfortably. On light/green fills, switch to #6F9E2D (--ink-accent). Clay #E8995C is for marks and large/mono text, not small body copy. Status colours are for UI states only — keep them out of brand layouts.
