**In short:** AI makes one step of the work faster. The process still runs at the pace of its slowest step, and that is usually a person's decision. Find that step before you buy anything.

## The bottleneck moves

A founder sets up an AI assistant to draft the monthly investor update. Two days become an hour. The board call still happens on the same day, debates the same numbers and puts off the same calls.

Every process has a slowest step. Speed up any other step and the queue in front of the slow one just gets longer.

Faros AI, which sells engineering analytics, studied telemetry from over 10,000 developers in a 2025 report. On teams with high AI use, developers completed 21% more tasks and merged 98% more pull requests, but the time spent reviewing those pull requests rose 91%. Its reading: the gains stall at human approval ([Faros AI](https://www.faros.ai/blog/ai-software-engineering)).

## Redesign the workflow, not just the task

In McKinsey's survey, out of 25 attributes tested, redesigning workflows had the biggest effect on whether organisations saw an EBIT impact from generative AI. Yet only 21% of respondents whose organisations used it said they had fundamentally redesigned at least some workflows ([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-how-organizations-are-rewiring-to-capture-value)).

Ask which decision the work feeds, who makes it and what the slowest step in front of it is.

Often the fix is not technical. Move the meeting. Let a named person approve spend below a set amount. Agree in advance which number triggers action, such as runway under a set number of months, so the board call confirms rather than debates.

## Count days, not impressions

Count days from month-end close to a decision, not time saved on one step. Feelings are a poor guide. In a 2025 trial by METR, experienced developers using AI tools took 19% longer to finish tasks, yet believed afterwards that AI had sped them up by 20% ([METR](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/)). METR's 2026 update says newer tools probably do speed developers up, but it still treats self-reported gains as unreliable ([METR](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/)).

## Leave some steps slow

A senior hire, a large contract or a price change deserves full attention, and more AI-drafted options can make it worse. If a decision happens once a quarter, saving a day on the draft may not matter.
