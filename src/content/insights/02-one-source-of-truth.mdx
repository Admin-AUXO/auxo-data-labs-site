Every tidy report built on its own copy of the data is one more version of the truth to defend. Agree the figure once, upstream, and let every report read from the same place. Five neat spreadsheets that quietly disagree are worse than one that everyone trusts — because the argument moves from the boardroom to the inbox, and never quite ends.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — one source the whole business agrees on.
