The global standards now put sustainability disclosure on the same footing as the financials — same governance, same audit-grade scrutiny, same investor reading it line by line. For property, where buildings carry most of the energy and emissions, that lands hard. The teams that get ahead stop treating ESG as a separate annual scramble and start sourcing it the way they source revenue: once, traceably, from systems of record.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — board- and investor-ready numbers, including the ones regulators are about to ask for.
