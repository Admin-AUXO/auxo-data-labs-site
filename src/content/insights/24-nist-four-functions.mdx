AI governance sounds like a project that needs a committee. It doesn't need to start as one. The NIST AI Risk Management Framework distils it to four functions — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — that any team can adopt without licence or consultant. Govern sets who's accountable; Map inventories what's running; Measure tests it; Manage acts on what you find. Using a recognised framework also travels: a regulator, auditor, or investor recognises the language immediately. Borrow the spine, then fit it to how you already operate.
