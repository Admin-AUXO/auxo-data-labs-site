If your team can't run it the morning after the consultant leaves, you didn't buy a capability — you rented a presentation. Ownership means the system, the documentation, and the know-how all stay with you. No retainer to renew, no call to place every time something needs changing. That's the whole point of the work: to leave you more capable than you were, not more dependent.

Where this fits: [how we work](/services/) — every engagement leaves you owning the system, with no retainer to renew.
