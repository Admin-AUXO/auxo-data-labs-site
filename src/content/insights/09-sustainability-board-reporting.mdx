The ISSB's IFRS S1 and S2 now put sustainability disclosure on the same footing as the financials — same governance, same audit-grade scrutiny, same investor reading it line by line. With more than twenty jurisdictions adopting and 2025 the first full reporting year, this is no longer a future problem. For property, where buildings carry roughly 40% of global energy use and emissions, it lands especially hard. The teams that get ahead stop treating ESG as a separate annual scramble and start sourcing it the way they source revenue: once, traceably, from systems of record.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — board- and investor-ready numbers, including the ones regulators are about to ask for.
