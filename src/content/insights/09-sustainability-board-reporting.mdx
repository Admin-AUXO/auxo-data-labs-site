For years, sustainability figures sat in a booklet nobody audited. The ISSB standards, IFRS S1 and S2, treat them as information investors use to decide where money goes. They took effect for reporting periods from 1 January 2024, and each country decides whether to adopt them. Even where they don't apply yet, large customers and lenders ask similar questions. The teams that cope well stop treating these numbers as a yearly scramble. They source them like revenue: once, traceably, from the systems of record.
