Valuation makes the headline, but cash makes the year. A forecast that tracks asset value and skips the timing of rent collection, service-charge recovery, and void periods is telling you how the portfolio looks, not how it runs. In Dubai, service charges now clear through RERA-approved budgets on the Mollak escrow system, so that line is both knowable and bounded. The operators who sleep at night model the money moving in and out, week by week — because that's the number that turns a good asset into a good year.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — forecasts grounded in the cash, not just the carrying value.
