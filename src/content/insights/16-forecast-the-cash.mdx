Revenue writes the headline. Cash decides whether the quarter works. A forecast that tracks bookings but waves through payment terms, late invoices and the timing of payroll and suppliers shows how the business looks in a photo, not how it behaves in motion. Most of these figures already sit in your billing and accounting systems, so they don't need to be guessed. Model the money moving in and out, week by week. That is the number that tells you how much runway you really have.
