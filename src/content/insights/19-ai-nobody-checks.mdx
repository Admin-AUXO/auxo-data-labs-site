Automation earns its keep where the work is repetitive and a mistake is cheap to undo. Where the stakes are real, keep a person signing off, and keep a clear record of what ran, on what data, and why. NIST's AI Risk Management Framework, which is free and voluntary, builds this into its Govern and Measure functions. It's also the version that survives an audit and the version your own team trusts. Unchecked AI doesn't save time. It postpones the day someone has to explain what it did.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). AI agents carry the routine work. A person approves what matters.
