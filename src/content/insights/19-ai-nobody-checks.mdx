Automation earns trust where the work is repetitive and the cost of a mistake is low. Put a person on the calls that carry real stakes, and keep a clear record of what ran, on what data, and why. The NIST AI Risk Management Framework calls this Govern and Measure — the unglamorous part that makes the rest defensible. That's the version that survives an audit, and the version your team actually trusts. Unwatched AI doesn't save time; it just defers the moment someone has to explain it.

Where this fits: [reliable automation](/services/) — software carries the routine work, with a person accountable for the calls that matter.
