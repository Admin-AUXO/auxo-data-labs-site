Not all AI carries the same weight. A tool that drafts an email is one thing; a tool that screens a tenant or scores a buyer's creditworthiness is another — that's the category regulators scrutinise most, and its reach increasingly follows the customer across borders, not the company. The practical move is to sort your AI by consequence: light-touch where the output is a suggestion, full governance and a human decision where it decides who gets in.

Where this fits: [reliable automation](/services/) — AI used only where it earns its place, with a person on the high-stakes calls.
