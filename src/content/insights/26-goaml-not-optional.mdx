Real estate firms are treated as designated non-financial businesses under the UAE's AML regime — which means registering on goAML, appointing a compliance officer, screening clients, checking who really owns the buyer, and filing the right reports are baseline obligations, not nice-to-haves. The 2025 framework refresh raised both the bar and the penalties: administrative fines now reach AED 5 million per violation, alongside licence suspension, and inspections now reach small and mid-sized firms. The teams that stay calm are the ones whose systems produce this evidence automatically.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — the obligations met as a by-product of how you already operate.
