Before you buy another platform, open the cupboards. Your CRM knows the customers. Accounting knows the money. Your HR system knows the people, and your engineering tools know what was built and shipped. The raw material isn't missing. It has just never been connected. A new platform resets the clock, retrains everyone and bets the year on a migration. The quieter win is connecting what you already pay for, so it finally talks and your AI tools can read it.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Built from the systems you already run, and yours to keep.
