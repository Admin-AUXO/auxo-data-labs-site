Everyone budgets for the AI model and forgets the cleanup that comes first. The model is rarely the hard part. The hard part is connecting systems that were never built to talk to each other, and agreeing which record is right. Skip it and even a strong model gives wrong answers quickly. Do it once, cleaned, agreed and connected, and every project after it gets faster. The foundation isn't the dull part of the work. It's the part that makes the rest possible.

Where this fits: [Analyze](/how-it-works/). Find what needs cleaning before you spend on AI.
