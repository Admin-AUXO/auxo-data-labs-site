takeaway: "Most teams already hold the data they need. The work is making the systems agree, so one figure means one thing."

---

Nobody's numbers are wrong, exactly. They just don't match. Sales reports one revenue figure, finance books another, and the board hears a third. That isn't a shortage of data. It is three systems that never agreed to mean the same thing. Collecting more won't fix it. Making the systems you already run reconcile will. Hand-fixing at month-end is the symptom. Agreement built in upstream is the cure, and it is also what any AI tool needs before its answers can be trusted.
