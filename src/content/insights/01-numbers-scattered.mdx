Nobody's numbers are wrong, exactly. They just don't match. Leasing swears occupancy is 92%, finance files 88%, and the board hears a third figure delivered with total confidence. That's not a data problem — it's a treaty problem, three systems that never agreed to mean the same thing. Collecting more won't fix it; making the systems you already run reconcile will. Hand-reconciliation is the symptom you feel every month-end. Agreement built in upstream is the cure you stop having to think about.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — one source the whole business agrees on.
