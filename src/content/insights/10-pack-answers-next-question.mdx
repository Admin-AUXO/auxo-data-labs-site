The moment that decides a report isn't the meeting — it's the follow-up question afterwards. "Where did this come from?" "Does it tie to the audited figure?" "What changed since last quarter?" A pack that answers those in one click earns trust; one that triggers a week of reconciliation quietly loses it. As audit-grade standards extend to sustainability data, the traceability bar now covers numbers that used to live in a side deck. Build for the question behind the question, and diligence stops being an event.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — numbers that trace back to source and hold up under questions.
