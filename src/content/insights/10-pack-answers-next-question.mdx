The moment that tests a report isn't the meeting. It's the question twenty minutes later: where did this come from? Does it tie to the accounts? What changed since last quarter? A pack that answers those in one click earns trust on the spot. One that triggers a week of reconciliation loses it just as fast. Write for the question behind the question, and a funding round or an audit stops being an event you brace for. The answers are already there.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Numbers that trace back to source and hold up under questions.
