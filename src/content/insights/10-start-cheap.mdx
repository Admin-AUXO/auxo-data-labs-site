The first thing you automate shouldn't be the thing that ends up in front of a regulator. Earn trust on low-stakes, reversible work first — the reconciliations, the reminders, the routine packs — then move up as the track record builds. Confidence compounds. Start where it's cheap to be wrong, and by the time you reach the work that matters, the system has already proven it can be trusted.

Where this fits: [reliable automation](/services/) — starting on the routine work, with a person accountable for the calls that matter.
