Regulators expect records kept for years — five, under the UAE regime — and produced on demand, even on deals that fell through. If your answer to "show us the trail" is a project to reconstruct it, you didn't have a trail; you had a hope. The fix is to capture who did what, when, and on what basis as the work happens, so the evidence accumulates quietly in the background. The best audit prep is the one you never have to do.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — evidence captured as you operate, ready before anyone asks.
