When someone asks "show us how this decision was made", the answer should already exist. For high-risk systems, the EU AI Act lists logging of activity so results can be traced. Auditors, enterprise customers and investors increasingly expect the same of any AI that touches money or people. If your answer is a project to rebuild the trail, you didn't have a trail. Record who did what, when, on what data and with whose approval, as the work happens. The best audit prep is the one you never have to do.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Every agent run recorded, with the person who approved it.
