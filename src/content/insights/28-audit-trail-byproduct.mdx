Regulators expect records kept for years — five, under the UAE regime — and produced on demand, including on the deals that quietly fell apart. If your answer to "show us the trail" is a project to reconstruct it, you didn't have a trail. You had a hopeful guess and a long weekend ahead. The fix is to capture who did what, when, and on what basis as the work happens, so the evidence stacks up by itself in the background. The best audit prep is the one you never actually have to do.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — evidence captured as you operate, ready before anyone asks.
