AI budgets usually count the licence and forget the running cost. Agents read more, think in more steps and call more tools than a chat window. Anthropic's own engineering write-up reports that agents typically use about 4 times the tokens of a chat, and multi-agent systems about 15 times. That can be worth it for valuable work, and wasteful for simple work. So model usage like any other variable cost: by task, by volume, by month. Then pick the simplest setup that does the job.
