Service charges quietly decide net yield, yet they're often the line a forecast waves through. In Dubai they don't need to be guessed: under Law No. 6 of 2019, charges run on RERA-approved budgets through the Mollak escrow system, verifiable on the Service Charge Index — commonly AED 3 to 70-plus per square foot. Multi-year fixed budgets are now appearing too. That makes the number both knowable and defensible. Treat it as a modelled input tied to the public record, and your net figures stop drifting from reality.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — net numbers built on charges you can trace, not estimate.
