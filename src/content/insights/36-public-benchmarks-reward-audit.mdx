Most teams test an AI agent once, like it, and switch it on. Then a model update or a new data source changes its behaviour, and nobody notices until a customer does. The fix is a set of checks you own: real tasks, with known good answers, run every time something changes. Anthropic's guidance on evaluating agents says 20 to 50 simple tasks drawn from real failures is a good start. Keep those tests in your name, next to the agent. Then you can prove it still works, not just insist it does.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Agents handed over with their tests, and optional managed upkeep to keep them tuned.
