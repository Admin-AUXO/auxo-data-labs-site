The Gulf's real estate data is going public and continuous — the Smart Rental Index in Dubai, DARI in Abu Dhabi, IFRS-grade disclosure for investors. When anyone can check the benchmark, the value shifts to systems that can prove their own numbers reconcile to it. That's only possible if you own the system end to end: traceable inputs, an auditable trail, and documentation your team can stand behind. A black box can't reconcile to a public source on demand. A system you own and understand can — and that is increasingly the whole game.
