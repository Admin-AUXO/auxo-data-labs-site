Since January 2025, Dubai's Smart Rental Index has quietly turned your paperwork into a scoreboard. It rates buildings one to five stars across 60-plus criteria, drawn straight from the lease and transaction data you file — and it can untether rent from the postcode, so a sharp building in a quiet area now outscores a tired one on a famous street. The catch: whatever you submit feeds a public benchmark your tenants and rivals can read. Messy lease data no longer just slows your reporting. It lowers your rating, in daylight.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — clean, agreed inputs, because someone else is scoring them now.
