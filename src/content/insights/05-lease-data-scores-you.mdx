Dubai's Smart Rental Index, live since January 2025, replaced the old rent calculator with AI-driven, building-level star ratings — 1 to 5, scored against more than sixty criteria and refreshed from real transaction and Ejari data. The rating now decouples rent from the neighbourhood average: a high-spec building in a secondary area can command more. Whatever you file is the raw material the index reads. Messy or inconsistent lease data doesn't just slow your reporting; it feeds a public benchmark your tenants and counterparties can check.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — clean, agreed inputs, because someone else is now scoring them too.
