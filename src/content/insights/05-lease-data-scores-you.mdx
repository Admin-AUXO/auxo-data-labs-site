Workplace AI assistants answer from the data each person is already allowed to open. That sounds safe until you look at how files are actually shared. The salary sheet in an open folder. The board pack shared with the whole company. Before, nobody found them. Now anyone can ask. Microsoft's own deployment blueprint for Copilot starts with fixing oversharing. The lesson holds for any assistant or agent: tidy who can see what first, then switch AI on.

Where this fits: [Analyze](/how-it-works/). Check access and data readiness before AI goes live.
