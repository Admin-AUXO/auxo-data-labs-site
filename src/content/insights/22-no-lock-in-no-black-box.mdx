Lock-in isn't only commercial — it's also when nobody on your side can explain how the thing works. A model you can't open, a pipeline only the vendor understands: that's a black box you happen to be paying for. Real ownership means you can trace any number back to its source and explain it to a regulator or an investor without a phone call. If you can't see inside it, you don't really own it.

Where this fits: [how we work](/services/) — systems you can see into, explain, and run yourself.
