The rent benchmark in Dubai is no longer a once-a-year zone average; since 2025 it's a continuously refreshed, building-level reference scored on 60-plus criteria. That cuts both ways for a forecast. Lean on it and your assumptions gain a credible, public anchor a counterparty can check. Ignore it and the same source becomes the number used to argue you down. Either way, a forecast that reconciles to the public benchmark is far harder to dismiss across the table.
