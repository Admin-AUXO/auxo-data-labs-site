takeaway: "A forecast built only on internal hopes is easy to argue down. Tie the key assumptions to sources an investor or lender can check for themselves."
order: 14
---

Every forecast rests on a few assumptions that do most of the work: how fast you hire, how many customers renew, what prices do, what a supplier will charge. If those come from gut feel, the first sharp question takes them apart. If each one points to a source someone else can check, such as your own history, a signed contract or a published benchmark, the forecast is much harder to wave away. Name the source next to each assumption. It takes minutes and saves meetings.
