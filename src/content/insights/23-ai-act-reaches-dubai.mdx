"We're not an EU company" is no longer the end of the sentence. Article 2 of the EU AI Act covers providers and deployers based outside the EU where the output of their AI system is used in the EU. A team in Dubai, New York or London can be in scope without an office in Brussels. If you have European customers, users or staff, the rules follow your output, not your address. Map where your AI's results land before you grow, not after.
