The expensive part of compliance isn't the filing — it's the scramble before every deadline, rebuilding the same evidence by hand. FATF's risk-based approach assumes controls are part of how a business runs, not a quarterly event bolted on top. Make the evidence a by-product of how you already operate, and the deadline stops being something your best people dread. The goal isn't to work harder near the cut-off; it's to arrive at the cut-off with the work already done, sitting where anyone can find it.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — stay ready for regulators without pulling your team off real work.
