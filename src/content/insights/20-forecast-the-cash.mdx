topic: "Forecasting"
title: "Forecast the cash, not just the value."
takeaway: "Asset value looks good on a slide. Service charges, voids, and collection timing are what actually decide whether the year works."
order: 20
---

Valuation makes the headline, but cash makes the year. A forecast that tracks asset value and skips the timing of rent collection, service-charge recovery, and void periods is telling you how the portfolio looks, not how it runs. The operators who sleep at night model the money moving in and out — week by week — because that's the number that determines whether a good asset becomes a good year.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — forecasts grounded in the cash, not just the carrying value.
