title: "Forecasts are for planning, not for promising."
takeaway: "A range you can defend to an investor beats a single confident number you can't. Show the assumptions, and the conversation changes."
order: 13
---

A single confident number invites an argument you can't win, because everyone knows a point estimate will be wrong. A range you can defend, with the assumptions in plain view, turns a forecast into a planning tool. The honesty is the point, not the apology. Investors and boards trust the team that shows its working far more than the one hoping its single number lands. The same goes for any forecast an AI tool produces: ask what it assumed. Forecast to plan, not to promise.
