Most reporting fights aren't really about numbers. They're about words. Does "occupancy" count signed leases or paid ones? Is a unit "available" the day it empties or the day it's listed? Give two teams the same term and two definitions, and no dashboard on earth will reconcile them — it'll just render the disagreement in higher resolution, faster. The unglamorous move wins every time: agree the definitions, write them down, and let the data fall in line behind the words. Vocabulary first. Visualisation later.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — one agreed definition, so one figure means one thing.
