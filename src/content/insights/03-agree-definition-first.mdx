Most reporting fights aren't about wrong numbers — they're about words. Does occupancy count signed leases or paid ones? Is a unit "available" the day it's vacated or the day it's listed? When two teams define the same term differently, no dashboard will reconcile them; it just renders the disagreement faster. With Ejari registration now the backbone of Dubai's rental data, the definition you pick upstream is the one the market reads downstream. Settle the definitions first, write them down, and the data falls in line behind them.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — one agreed definition, so one figure means one thing.
