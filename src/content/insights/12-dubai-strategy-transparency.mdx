title: "Due diligence is a data test."
takeaway: "Whether you are raising, selling or signing a large customer, the other side will ask for numbers. How fast you can trace them says a lot."
order: 12
---

Every funding round, acquisition and large contract ends in the same place: a data room and a list of questions. How many customers renewed? What does each one cost to serve? Does the forecast tie to the accounts? A team with one traced source answers in a day. A team stitching spreadsheets answers in weeks, and the delay itself becomes a concern. Get the data ready before anyone asks, and due diligence becomes a check, not a crisis.

Where this fits: [Analyze](/how-it-works/). See where your numbers would hold up, and where they wouldn't.
