Dubai's Real Estate Sector Strategy 2033, announced by DLD in late 2024, aims to roughly double the sector's GDP contribution and lift market value toward AED 1 trillion — and it names transparency through data insights as its first pillar. When the regulator's own thesis is that better data wins capital, reporting that can't be traced or explained stops looking conservative and starts looking like risk. The market is being built to reward institutions whose numbers reconcile to the public record. Report to that standard before a counterparty makes you.
