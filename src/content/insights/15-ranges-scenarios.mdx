---

One line on a chart tells you what someone hopes. A base case, a downside, and a note on what would have to be true for each tell you what to do when things change, which they will. This isn't hedging. It answers the question that matters before you commit money or people: what breaks this plan, and how would we see it coming? It works for a hiring plan, a cash runway or a product launch alike. Plan in ranges. Test with scenarios.
