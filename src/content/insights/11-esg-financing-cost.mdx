Climate disclosure stopped being a reputational exercise the moment it started moving money. Under IFRS S2, physical and transition risk feed directly into how an asset is valued, insured, and financed — lenders and underwriters now read the same numbers your board does. For a property portfolio, an inefficient building isn't just an emissions line; it's a higher cost of capital and a steeper insurance quote. Treating ESG as a financial input, sourced once and traceably, is cheaper than discovering the gap at refinancing.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — climate numbers sourced like financials, because lenders read them that way.
