In March 2024 the US SEC settled charges against two investment advisers for false and misleading statements about their use of AI. The SEC chair called it "AI washing". The lesson reaches well beyond finance. Every claim about AI in a deck, on a website or in an investor update is a statement someone may check. Describe what the AI actually does, on what data, and who approves its output. If you can't show it working, don't claim it.
