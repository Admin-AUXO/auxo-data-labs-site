Saudi Arabia just changed the price of standing still. A 5% Real Estate Transaction Tax now applies under the 2025 RETT Law; the renamed White Land and Vacant Real Estate Tax reaches idle buildings, not just bare plots above 5,000 m², at 5% scaling to 10%; and a fresh foreign-ownership regime adds fees on non-Saudi transfers from 2026. For anyone modelling a Saudi position, these aren't footnotes — they reset the carry, move the breakeven, and quietly demolish the case for sitting on land and hoping. Price the tax in, or the tax prices you.
