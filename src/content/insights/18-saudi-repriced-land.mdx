Rules on AI and automated decisions now arrive with dates attached. California's privacy regulator says businesses that use automated decision-making technology to make significant decisions must meet its new requirements from 1 January 2027. Other places have their own timelines. Each one means work: listing the tools, writing notices, checking how decisions are made. That work takes people and weeks. Put the dates in the plan and the budget now, so the deadline is a checkpoint and not a scramble.

Where this fits: [Advise](/how-it-works/). Governance planned around the dates that apply to you.
