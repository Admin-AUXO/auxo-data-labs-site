Saudi Arabia has reset the cost of holding and trading real estate. The Real Estate Transaction Tax applies at 5% under the 2025 RETT Law; the renamed White Land and Vacant Real Estate Tax now reaches vacant buildings, not just bare plots over 5,000 m², at 5% scaling to 10%; and a new foreign-ownership regime adds fees on non-Saudi transfers from 2026. For anyone forecasting a Saudi position, these aren't footnotes — they change the carry, the breakeven, and the case for sitting on land at all.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — forecasts that price in the tax regime, not just the upside.
