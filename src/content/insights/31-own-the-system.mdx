If your team can't run it the morning after the outside help leaves, you didn't buy a capability. You rented a convincing presentation. Real ownership means the code, the setup, the accounts, the documentation and the know-how all stay on your side, in your company's name. No retainer to renew, no support ticket every time a number or an agent rule needs changing. The point of doing the work properly is to leave you more capable, not more dependent.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Set up end to end and handed over. Built to last, and yours to keep.
