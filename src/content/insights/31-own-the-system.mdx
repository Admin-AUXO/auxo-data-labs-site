If your team can't run it the morning after the consultant leaves, you didn't buy a capability — you rented a presentation. Ownership means the system, the documentation, and the know-how all stay with you. NIST's framework makes the point in governance terms: accountability and traceability have to live inside the organisation that runs the system, not the vendor that built it. No retainer to renew, no call to place every time something needs changing. That's the whole point of the work: to leave you more capable, not more dependent.
