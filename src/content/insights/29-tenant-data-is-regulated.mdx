Most teams under-rate how much personal data they hold. CRM histories, payroll files, CVs and support tickets are all personal data. In the UAE, the Personal Data Protection Law covers processing inside or outside the country, with rules on consent and cross-border transfer. In Europe and the UK, the GDPR sets similar duties. When an AI tool reads that data, those rules come with it. Know what personal data you hold, why, and who and what can touch it, before you connect an AI tool to it.
