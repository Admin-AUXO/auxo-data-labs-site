The UAE's Personal Data Protection Law treats the information you hold on tenants and buyers — KYC documents, Ejari records, CRM histories, screening results — as personal data, with obligations on how it's stored, shared, and retained. It reaches processing inside or outside the UAE where the data subject is here. The Executive Regulations that switch on the penalty mechanics are still pending, which makes this the moment to get the basics right cheaply: know what personal data you hold, why, and who can touch it — before enforcement, not after.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — personal data handled to standard, captured in how you already operate.
