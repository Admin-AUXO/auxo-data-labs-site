Here's a category most property teams under-rate: the files on their tenants and buyers. Under the UAE's Personal Data Protection Law, KYC documents, Ejari records, CRM histories and screening results are all personal data — with real rules on how you store, share and dispose of them, reaching anyone in the country wherever you happen to process it. The Executive Regulations that switch on the penalties are still pending, which makes right now the cheap moment to get the basics straight: know what personal data you hold, why, and who can touch it — before enforcement asks, not after.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — personal data handled to standard, captured in how you already operate.
