A rip-and-replace sounds decisive and usually isn't. It resets the clock, retrains the team, and bets the outcome on a migration. The quieter win is to build on the systems you already run — your PMS, your accounting, your records, the public sources like DLD and Ejari you already reconcile against — and make them work together. You keep the tools your people know, and a new platform stays the last resort, not the first move. Capability you can keep beats a migration you have to survive.
