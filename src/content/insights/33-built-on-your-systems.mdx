Rip-and-replace sounds decisive in the meeting and rarely is in practice. It resets the clock, retrains everyone, and stakes the outcome on a migration going smoothly. The better win is usually to build on what you already run, your CRM, your accounting, your HR and engineering tools, and make them work together. If an AI tool or agent you already have works, keep it. Your people keep the tools they know, and a new platform becomes the last resort instead of the first move.
