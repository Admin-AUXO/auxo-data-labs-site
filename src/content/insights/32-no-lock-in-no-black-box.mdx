Lock-in isn't only the contract you can't escape. It's the quieter trap where nobody on your side can actually explain how the thing works — a model you can't open, a pipeline only the vendor understands. NIST treats explainability as a core property of a trustworthy system for exactly this reason. Real ownership means you can trace any number back to its source and explain it to a regulator or an investor without first dialling a consultant. If you can't see inside it, you don't own it. You're just paying rent on the mystery.
