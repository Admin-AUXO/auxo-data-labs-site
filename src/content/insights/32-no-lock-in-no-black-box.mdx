Lock-in isn't only the contract you can't leave. It's the quieter trap where nobody on your side can explain how the thing works: an agent whose rules only the vendor knows, a pipeline nobody internal has read. NIST's AI Risk Management Framework treats explainability as part of what makes AI trustworthy. Real ownership means you can trace any number or AI output back to its source and explain it to an auditor or investor without calling anyone. If you can't see inside it, you don't own it.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Systems you can see into, explain and run yourself.
