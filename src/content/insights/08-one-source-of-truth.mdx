Every tidy report built on its own copy of the data is one more version of the truth to defend. Agree the figure once, upstream — ideally at the registered source, like Ejari — and let every report read from the same place. Five neat spreadsheets that quietly disagree are worse than one that everyone trusts, because the argument moves from the boardroom to the inbox and never quite ends. The goal isn't more reports. It's one number nobody has to reconcile twice.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — one source the whole business agrees on.
