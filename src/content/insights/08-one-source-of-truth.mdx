Five tidy reports, each built on its own copy of the data, are five versions of the truth. They agree until the quarter that matters. Then they split, and the argument moves into a long email thread that never quite ends. One number everyone reads from beats five neat ones nobody fully trusts. Agree the figure once, upstream, and point every report and every AI tool at the same place. Reconciliation should be a design choice made once, not a monthly ritual.
