Before anyone proposes a new platform, look at what you already have. Your property and accounting systems hold the operational truth; public records like Ejari and the DLD platforms hold the registered one. The opportunity isn't a fresh data lake — it's wiring the systems you already pay for so they reconcile against each other. The win is connection, not another platform to buy.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — built from the systems you already run, and yours to keep.
