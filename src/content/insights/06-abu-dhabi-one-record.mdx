Abu Dhabi settled the "whose number is right" argument by simply publishing the answer. DARI, the emirate's official real-estate ecosystem, is the single record for sales, leases, Tawtheeq tenancy registrations, title deeds and the licensed-broker directory, verified by the emirate's municipalities department and refreshed daily. Only approved projects and real professionals appear, which makes it an anti-fraud layer as much as a registry. The implication for your spreadsheet is blunt: the authoritative figure now lives outside it. Smart operators reconcile to DARI continuously, instead of meeting the gap when a counterparty waves it across the table.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — internal numbers that reconcile to the public record by design.
