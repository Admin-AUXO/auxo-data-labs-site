Abu Dhabi's DARI platform, run by the Department of Municipalities and Transport, is now the unified record for buying, selling, leasing, Tawtheeq documentation, title deeds, and the registered-broker directory. Only approved projects and licensed professionals appear, which makes it an anti-fraud transparency layer as much as a registry. For an operator, that changes the burden of proof: the authoritative number lives outside your spreadsheet. The teams that stay clean reconcile their internal figures against DARI continuously, rather than discovering the gap when a counterparty does.

Where this fits: [trusted data](/services/) — internal numbers that reconcile to the public record by design.
