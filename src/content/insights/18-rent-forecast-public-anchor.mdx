The rent benchmark in Dubai is no longer a once-a-year zone average; it's a continuously refreshed, building-level reference. That cuts both ways for a forecast. Lean on it and your assumptions gain a credible, public anchor a counterparty can check. Ignore it and the same source becomes the number used to argue you down. Either way, a forecast that reconciles to the public benchmark is harder to dismiss.

Where this fits: [clear reporting](/services/) — forecasts anchored to references your counterparties already trust.
