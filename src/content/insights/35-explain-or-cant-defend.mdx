The hardest moment for any number isn't producing it. It's being asked, live, exactly how you got it. Regulators are pushing the same way. The UK's data protection regulator, the ICO, builds its AI guidance around accountability, transparency and accuracy. In practice, every figure or AI output you put in front of a lender, an auditor or a board should trace back to its source and its logic. A confident answer you can't unpack is a liability. A traceable one ends the conversation.

Where this fits: [Build](/how-it-works/). Systems where every number and every AI output traces back to its source.
