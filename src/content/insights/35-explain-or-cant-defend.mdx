The hardest moment for any number isn't producing it. It's being asked, live and under pressure, exactly how you got it. The EU AI Act and audit-grade reporting standards now push the same direction: high-stakes outputs have to be explainable and traceable, not merely plausible and delivered with a straight face. For a property business, that means every figure you put in front of a lender, regulator or board should trace cleanly back to its source and its logic. A confident answer you can't unpack is a liability. A traceable one ends the conversation.
