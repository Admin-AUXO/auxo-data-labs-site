The hardest moment for any number isn't producing it — it's being asked, under pressure, exactly how it was produced. Both the EU AI Act and audit-grade reporting standards now push the same way: high-stakes outputs must be explainable and traceable, not just plausible. For a property business, that means any figure put in front of a regulator, lender, or board should trace cleanly back to its source and its logic. A confident answer you can't unpack is a liability. A traceable one ends the conversation.
