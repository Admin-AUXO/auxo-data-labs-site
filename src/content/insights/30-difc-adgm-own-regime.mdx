A developer or family office with an entity inside DIFC or ADGM is governed by that zone's own data-protection law — both GDPR-modelled, both stricter in places than the federal regime — not the UAE PDPL. ADGM, for instance, requires breach notification within 72 hours. The trap is the data that moves between a free-zone entity and a mainland one: two regimes, one flow, and obligations that don't automatically line up. The practical first step is a data map — what's held where, under which law — before a transfer creates a problem.

Where this fits: [confident compliance](/services/) — obligations mapped across zones, not assumed to be one.
