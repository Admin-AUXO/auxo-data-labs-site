A team in London, a vendor in California and an office in Dubai can mean three sets of data rules on one customer record. Personal data leaving Europe needs a transfer tool, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. The UAE's data law has its own rules on cross-border transfer. AI tools add another hop, because prompts and files often go to a model hosted somewhere else. The plain first step is a data map: what is held where, under which law, and which tools send it on.

Where this fits: [Advise](/how-it-works/). Data flows mapped, so every tool fits the rules that apply.
