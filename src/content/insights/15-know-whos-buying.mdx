When a corporate buyer signs, the entity on the contract is the easy part. The question that matters is who ultimately owns it, and whether that person clears sanctions and watchlist screening — answered before the deal closes, not after a regulator asks. Done manually, it's the step everyone rushes. Built into onboarding, it's a box that ticks itself and a record that's already there when someone comes looking.
