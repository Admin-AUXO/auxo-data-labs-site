The first thing you automate should not be the thing that ends up in front of a regulator. Start where being wrong is cheap and reversible — the reconciliations, the reminders, the routine packs — and let a track record build before you hand over anything that bites back. Confidence compounds: by the time you reach the work that genuinely matters, the system has already earned the benefit of the doubt. Begin where a mistake costs an apology, not a licence. Ambition is fine. Sequence it.

Where this fits: [reliable automation](/services/) — starting on the routine work, with a person accountable for the calls that matter.
